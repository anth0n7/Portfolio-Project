# Portfolio

Anthony Thomas's personal site. It exists to get recruiters to open the resume or a project within a 30-second skim, and to leave room for freelance work later.

## Audience

**Recruiter**:
The primary visitor: a recruiter or hiring manager evaluating Anthony, a May 2026 graduate, for entry-level SWE roles.
_Avoid_: Employer, HR

**Client**:
A future, secondary visitor looking to hire Anthony for freelance work. The site is not built for Clients yet.
_Avoid_: Customer

## Content

**Project**:
A piece of software Anthony built and shows on the site, presented as a card on the home page.
_Avoid_: Work item, repo

**Case Study**:
The dedicated page for a single Project. It tells the problem, approach, architecture, hard parts and results.
_Avoid_: Project page, detail page, write-up
